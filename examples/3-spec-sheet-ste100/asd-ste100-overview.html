Write 2 instructions in 1 sentence only when you do the 2 steps at the same time. Count a number and its unit as 1 word. Also count a hyphenated word and text in parentheses as 1 word. In a vertical list, a colon counts as a full stop.

Verb forms

Section 3
FormExample
Imperative (command)Stop the fan.
Simple presentThe fan stops.
Simple pastThe fan stopped.
Simple futureThe fan will stop.
InfinitiveUse a key to stop it.
Past participle as adjectivethe stopped fan
Progressive, perfectis stopping, has stopped
Passive in an instructionIt must be stopped.
-ing form as a verbbefore stopping it

Use the -ing form only in a technical noun, for example "landing gear". In descriptive text, use the passive only when the agent is unknown.

UPPERCASE: approved. Lowercase: not approved. An approved word keeps only its approved meaning and part of speech. Technical nouns and verbs are not in the dictionary. Do not use a technical noun as a verb (rule 1.7) or a technical verb as a noun (rule 1.13).

F

History and status

asd-ste100.org
  1. 1979Airlines and AECMA start the project
  2. 1986First AECMA guide
  3. 2004New name: ASD-STE100
  4. 2025Issue 9: a standard

ATA iSpec 2200 and directives from EASA, FAA and CAAC make STE mandatory for aircraft manuals. S1000D recommends it. Writers also use STE for defense, vehicles, energy and medical devices. The STEMG schedules Issue 10 for January 2028.
